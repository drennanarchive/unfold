Team Operating System

A living operating model for a leader and their team. Stand up the organization, run the operating rhythm, and continuously improve how work gets done.

The Leadership Contract

The leader provides direction, decisions, priorities, and protection. The team provides analysis, recommendations, transparency, and execution reality.

The goal is not perfect execution. The goal is a team that knows why it exists, how it operates, and how to adapt.
