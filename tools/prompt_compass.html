WORD FINDER Help me find the design language +
Editorial

Strong type, deliberate whitespace, fewer but larger compositional moves.

App-like utility

Obvious controls, fast state changes, little decorative friction.

Immersive / cinematic

Atmosphere, depth, scene-like composition. Better for lingering than speed.

Tactile

Controls feel pressable, selected, dragged, completed, or settled.

Quiet hierarchy

One clear focal point; secondary choices stay visible but subordinate.

Soft-tech

Modern and digital without enterprise coldness: refined type, gentle depth, controlled motion.

Separate what you know from what you are inferring.
